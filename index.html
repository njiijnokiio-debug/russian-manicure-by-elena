The technique

The Art of Russian Manicure

Russian manicure is a technique built around careful preparation. The focus is on the cuticle line, accurate shaping and a smooth, even surface — so the nail looks neat up close and the polish sits cleanly.

  1. 01

    Preparation

    Careful work with the nail plate and cuticle area before styling.

  2. 02

    Cuticle work

    Detailed, precise cuticle work for a clean, defined line.

  3. 03

    Shaping

    An even, balanced shape that follows the natural nail.

  4. 04

    Finish

    A smooth, polished surface and a neat final look.
